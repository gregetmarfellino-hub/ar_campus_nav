AR Campus Nav — sample QR anchors

Open any PNG on a second screen (PC/laptop) and scan it from the app.
These are development markers for the demo venue only.

Payload format:
  arnav://anchor?venue=<venue-id>&id=<anchor-id>

Included anchors:
  main_entrance.png
  main_park_bend.png
  electronics_junction.png
  terrace_entrance.png
  road_entry.png

The QR does NOT contain the whole map. It identifies a venue + anchor.
The installed venue package supplies the node, floor, and optional heading metadata.
